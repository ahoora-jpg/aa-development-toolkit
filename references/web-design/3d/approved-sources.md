Approved uses:

- One homepage Hero
- One featured-product presentation
- Lightweight ambient geometry
- Mouse-responsive product rotation
- Optional showroom experiment

Restrictions:

- Do not render 3D on every page.
- Do not hide primary content inside Canvas.
- Provide a static fallback image.
- Disable or simplify on weak devices.
- Lazy-load the 3D module.
- Pause rendering outside the viewport.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Optimize models and textures.

Initial Safari Home concept:

A single premium appliance model or abstract home-appliance geometry placed beside the main Hero text, with subtle pointer movement and restrained lighting.

Decision:
Approved only for a controlled prototype.
