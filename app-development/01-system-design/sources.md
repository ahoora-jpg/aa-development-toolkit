# System Design Sources

Verified 2026-07-24.

- [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) — Widely used, actively maintained open-source survey of distributed-system concepts and design tradeoffs.
- [Google Site Reliability Engineering book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) — Primary production guidance from Google on SLOs, overload, cascading failures, capacity, and reliability.
- [AWS Builders' Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/) — First-party articles describing operational lessons from large distributed systems.
- [Awesome Software Architecture](https://github.com/mehdihadeli/awesome-software-architecture) — Actively maintained, clearly licensed index used to cross-check terminology and deeper references.
