# Content Strategy & Copywriting

## Purpose

How to write website copy that gets a visitor to actually take action (buy, contact, subscribe) — not just fill space. The best design in the world underperforms if the words on it are vague, generic, or written for the writer instead of the reader. Works alongside `skills/seo/` (findability) and `skills/marketing/conversion-rate-optimization/` (turning that attention into action).

https://baymard.com

## Core Concepts To Apply

- **Write for scanners, not readers**: most visitors skim rather than read line-by-line — lead every section with the key point in the heading itself, use short paragraphs, and bullet lists so a scanning visitor still gets the message.
- **Benefits over features**: state what a feature means for the customer ("keeps food fresh for a week longer") before or alongside the raw spec ("no-frost compressor") — a features-only description forces the reader to do the translation themselves, and most won't bother.
- **One clear next step per page**: every page should point toward a single primary action (buy, call, visit the store) — competing calls-to-action dilute conversion, covered further in `skills/marketing/conversion-rate-optimization/`.
- **Match content format to intent**: a page for someone comparing options needs a different structure (comparison, specs, reviews) than a page for someone ready to buy (clear price, stock status, fast checkout path) — write the format the visitor actually needs at that decision stage, not a generic "About this product" block.
- **Real, specific proof over vague claims**: a specific number, a named customer detail, or an actual before/after outperforms generic superlatives ("the best quality") — vague praise reads as filler and is ignored by both readers and, increasingly, AI systems evaluating content quality (see `skills/seo/generative-engine-optimization/`).
- **Consistent brand voice everywhere**: the tone defined during `skills/design/branding/` work should carry through product pages, emails, and any AI-assisted drafts — edit AI-generated copy for real voice and specific detail rather than publishing it as-is.
- **Answer objections before they're asked**: shipping cost, return policy, warranty, and durability are the most common hesitations for a home-appliance purchase — address them directly on the product page rather than making the visitor go find that information elsewhere.

## When To Use

Write or rewrite page copy after brand voice (`skills/design/branding/`) is settled and before final visual design is locked — copy that has to be squeezed into an already-finished layout is usually weaker than copy the layout was built around.
